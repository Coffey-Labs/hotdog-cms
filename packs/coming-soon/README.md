# Coming soon pack

A holding page for a site that isn't open yet, or is closed for a while:
your name, a headline, when you'll open, a few words, and optionally a form
from `forms/` (the contact-forms pack's mailing list sign-up fits) and
links. No menu, so there's nowhere to wander off to.

    hotdog-cms pack add coming-soon

Give the home page `layout: holding` until launch day.
