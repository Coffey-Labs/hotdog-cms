# Glossary pack

Terms and what they mean, sorted A to Z, with a row of letters to jump with.
Each term has its own link (`/glossary/#levain`) to send people straight to
it, and "See also" links between related terms. Search engines get the list
as a DefinedTermSet.

    hotdog-cms pack add glossary

Keep the terms in `data/glossary.yaml`, in any order, and add a Glossary
section with `from: glossary`.
