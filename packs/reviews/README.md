# Reviews pack

Customers' star ratings, with the average and how many there are: as a
section on any page, or under a product of the shop pack.

    hotdog-cms pack add reviews

Stars are text (★), with "4 out of 5 stars" for screen readers. A product's
reviews give search engines its rating (AggregateRating). A business's
reviews of itself give none, because search engines ignore a business's
ratings on its own site.
