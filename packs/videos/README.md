# Videos pack

A video library from your own files: a page per video with its player,
captions and transcript; `/videos/` with every poster; a feed of new videos;
and a "Latest videos" section for any page. Videos with a poster and a date
are described to search engines (VideoObject).

    hotdog-cms pack add videos

Nothing is embedded from YouTube or anywhere else, so nothing tracks your
visitors. For one video on a page, the starter's Video section is enough.
