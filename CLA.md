# HotDog CMS Contributor Agreement

Version 1.0

Thank you for contributing to HotDog CMS. This agreement sets out the terms
of your contribution to Coffey Labs LLC, an Arizona limited liability
company ("Coffey Labs"), which maintains the project. It's a license, not a
transfer: you keep the copyright in your work, and you can use it however
you like.

You accept it by following the steps in [How to sign](#how-to-sign). It then
covers every Contribution you make to the project from that point on.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the person or legal entity accepting this agreement. If
  you're an entity, "You" includes any entity that controls, is controlled
  by, or is under common control with you.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship (code, documentation,
  designs, templates, translations or anything else) that You submit to
  Coffey Labs for inclusion in HotDog CMS. That includes a pull request,
  patch, issue attachment or message on a project channel. Something you
  clearly mark "Not a Contribution" isn't one.
- **"Project"** means HotDog CMS and the other software and documentation
  that Coffey Labs maintains in the same repositories.

## 4. What Coffey Labs promises in return

- **It stays open source.** Coffey Labs will keep every Contribution it

- **Successors are bound.** If Coffey Labs transfers the Project, this

## 5. What You promise

- **It's Yours to give.** Each Contribution is Your original work, or You

  else's work, You say so in the Contribution, with its source and license.
- **Your employer agrees.** If an employer or anyone else has rights in
  what You create, You have their permission to make Contributions on their
  behalf under this agreement, or they have waived those rights for Your
  Contributions. An entity can sign for its employees: see

- **You'll tell us.** If You learn that any of this is no longer true, You
  will tell Coffey Labs at legal@coffeylabs.org.

- If any part of it is held unenforceable, the rest stays in effect.
- It's the whole agreement between You and Coffey Labs about Your

## How to sign

**As an individual:** in your first pull request, add a line to
[CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) with your name and the email address you
commit with, in a commit of its own whose message says:

    I agree to the HotDog CMS Contributor Agreement, version 1.0.

Sign off that commit (`git commit -s`). Your later pull requests need
nothing more.

**For an entity** (a company whose employees contribute as part of their
work): email legal@coffeylabs.org from someone who can bind the company.
Name the company, say that it accepts this agreement for its employees'
Contributions, and list the people and email addresses it covers. Each of
them then adds their line to CONTRIBUTORS.md with the company named.

Questions go to legal@coffeylabs.org.
